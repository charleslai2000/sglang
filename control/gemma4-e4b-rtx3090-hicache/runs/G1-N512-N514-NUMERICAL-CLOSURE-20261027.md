# G1 N512/N514 numerical closure — 2026-10-27

## Disposition

The frozen N512/N514 BF16-vs-HF guard remains **FAILED**; no production arithmetic defect or root cause is established. The current evidence is insufficient to call the frozen HF CPU values an implementation-independent numerical contract for RTX3090 SGLang. It is also insufficient to declare the guard formally incompatible with the product contract: effective post-load W4A16 weights and materialized masks have not been proven equivalent, and the first layer-level differences have not been isolated with identical operation inputs. No tolerance or production math is changed. G1 remains **NOT ACCEPTED**; an owner/architecture decision is required on the reference contract before promoting this comparison as an acceptance oracle.

Therefore the old artifact's declared generation route is CPU, but its observed logits are CUDA-identical; the historical runtime/device provenance of the stored vector is **UNKNOWN**. This discrepancy cannot be resolved from surviving records and must not be explained by inference. At N514, the old oracle matches the Oct 25 CPU vector (rel-L2 0) while CUDA differs (`.174377`), demonstrating the inconsistency in backend fingerprint across Ns; it does not establish a cause.
- Layer observations are localization, not diagnosis. - One tempting N514 suspect is specifically not supported: same-Forward owner23 cache-slot/KV capture and corrected HF replay show owner K/V close to the HF owner state (rel-L2 `.001237/.000136`); Triton pre-O attention heads agree with independent FP32 GQA at rel-L2 `.001881`; corrected HF consumer decoder output agrees at `.003815`. - Official-source text-only compatibility service and Fork SGLang produce elementwise-identical full logits for N8/N512/N514 under their matched service conditions. Exact effective post-load weight equality and serialized byte-equivalence of Full/SWA masks are absent. These are plausible differences, not demonstrated causes.
- GSM8K historical full-run configs also differ in realized scheduling: vLLM used `max_num_seqs=16`, while SGLang's corrected runs used C1; full batch traces are not retained. Matched stop strings and request prompt IDs do not establish identical generated histories or math-path equivalence. This is contextual, not evidence about the N512/514 numerical cause.

## First verifiable divergence and contract question

The first *observed* SGLang-vs-HF layer residual exists at layer0; it is not an identified first faulty computation. The first marked increase is around layer16/17, but without same-operation inputs and verified effective weight/mask identity it cannot be assigned to a SGLang operator. For N514, the later full-attention layer23 residual increase is real in the all-layer trace, while its isolated owner/cache/attention replay did not show the hypothesized attention defect. Therefore the requested “first verified cause” is **not currently established**.

Architecture/owner disposition needed: should the frozen HF CPU Eager logits, with compressed-tensors QAT execution, be a hard numerical acceptance oracle for SGLang Triton+Marlin on RTX3090, or only a cross-backend diagnostic pending a backend-matched reference contract?
