## RoPE lead checked and rejected

Raw HF `AutoConfig` initially shows `head_dim=256`, which made full-attention RoPE look suspect. The pinned config separately has `global_head_dim=512`, `rope_parameters.full_attention.partial_rotary_factor=0.25`, and SWA theta 10000. SGLang's `_apply_gemma4_text_attention_overrides()` saves SWA dimensions then normalizes `head_dim=512`; runtime `ModelConfig` resolves `(head_dim, swa_head_dim, v_head_dim, swa_v_head_dim)=(512,256,512,256)`. `Gemma4DecoderLayer` passes 512 to full attention and 256 to SWA; `get_rope` applies the 0.25 factor to the full dimension, yielding rotary_dim 128, and full `rope_theta=1,000,000`; SWA rotates its 256-d head with default theta 10,000. This agrees with pinned Transformers `Gemma4TextAttention`/position embeddings. The apparent wrong-RoPE-dimension lead was caused by checking raw, pre-normalization config and is disconfirmed; no RoPE math change is justified.
