## Source contract checked

- **Correction to the earlier RoPE suspicion:** checkpoint `gemma4_text` sets `head_dim=256` for SWA and `global_head_dim=512` for full attention; its full RoPE uses `partial_rotary_factor=0.25`, i.e. a 128-d rotary subspace, while SWA default RoPE rotates its 256-d head. The SGLang text-config normalizer (`_apply_gemma4_text_attention_overrides`) preserves SWA dimensions, then sets `config.head_dim=global_head_dim`; `ModelConfig` derives `head_dim=512`, `swa_head_dim=256`. `Gemma4DecoderLayer` passes 512 for full attention and 256 for SWA to `get_rope`, where the full factor reduces `rotary_dim` to 128. This matches the HF `Gemma4TextAttention` and position-embedding implementation; the initial apparent dimension mismatch came from reading raw HF config before SGLang normalization. No RoPE patch is indicated by this lead.
- **Actual layer-error localization:** exact-position BF16/HF last-token layer residual relative-L2 stays ~0.002–0.015 through layers 0–17 at N=512/514. At N=512 the all-sequence comparison has a localized outlier at layer20 (text-token index 503, max abs 9.5; layer21 max abs 16.75); the last-token residual remains ~0.013 through layer22 and rises to .0394 at full-attention layer23. This identifies layer23/full-attention as a material late divergence site for the last token at 514, but layer outputs alone do not yet distinguish expected numerical propagation, attention semantics, shared cache, or projection/MLP effects.
