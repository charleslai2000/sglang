Run: T012 paired CT-loader / Humming post-load qualification (2026-11-27)
Purpose: prove the unchanged compressed-tensors loader places the checkpoint's
packed INT4 words and BF16 group scales on every Text Core WNA16 layer, then
convert those very tensors to Humming post-load and check whole-model behaviour.

Arms: identical argv (no --quantization override). Only the post-load branch
differs: `SGLANG_CTWNA16_HUMMING=1` on the Humming arm.
Shared: Gemma4 checkpoint, BF16 compute, FP8-E5M2 KV, Triton attention,
Decode CUDA Graph bs<=32, FA2 disabled, radix cache disabled.

Audit (qualification only, env-gated, absent from the production hot path):
- `SGLANG_CTWNA16_AUDIT_DIR`: per-parameter SHA256 of `weight_packed` /
  `weight_scale` plus `weight_shape`, taken in `process_weights_after_loading`
  BEFORE Marlin repack or Humming transform, in both arms.
- `SGLANG_CTWNA16_AUDIT_GEMM`: for six named layers, an FP32 dequantized
  reference GEMM built from the same CT words/scales, compared with the
  converted layer's real GEMM output.

  humming::humming_gemm and Marlin linear dispatch per process, saves the first

  that a Humming kernel actually appears in the replayed graph.

Gates (compare_arms.py): identical pre-conversion SHA256 set, loaded element
counts equal to checkpoint totals, no missing Text Core WNA16 tensors, no CT
packed parameter left resident after conversion, converted GEMM within tolerance
of the segment reference and of Marlin, Humming-only dispatch with capture-phase
and replay kernel evidence, identical greedy tokens, matching logits.

Timeout: 600 s per arm startup gate, 1500 s hard wrapper timeout.
Outputs: marlin/, humming/, checkpoint-textcore.json, verdict.json, compare.log.
