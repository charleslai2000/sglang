“Busy union” is the union of Nsight CUPTI device Kernel intervals strictly intersected with each exact scheduler-step interval; it is not the sum of Kernel durations, and not an independently sampled occupancy. “SM Active”, “Tensor Active”, and DRAM `%Peak` are 1-ms **whole-device** Nsight GA10x counters averaged only over the exact same step interval; they are contextual hardware indicators, not per-RID resource attribution. ## How the L0 table was computed

The same-run Nsight SQLite contains 1,442,068 CUDA Kernel events, 195,081 CUDA Runtime events, 120,177 CUDA memcpy events, and 7,161 CUDA synchronization events, plus 127,105 unique 1-ms `GPU_METRICS` timestamps; median sampling spacing is 1,000,001 ns and there are no gaps above 2 ms. The GA10x metric set carries 31 hardware columns. For B/C/D the exact sample windows are Scheduler `scheduler_step_start` through same-iteration `scheduler_step_forward_submit_end`, transformed to Nsight session nanoseconds using the report's same-session monotonic origin. Counters reported are arithmetic means of samples wholly inside the window; n=255/311/343 samples. Metric extrema, included here as caveats rather than headline values: SM Active max 100%; DRAM Read `%Peak` max 98%; the corresponding minima are zero. Those spikes mean percentages are distributions, not constant rates.

Per-step Kernel interval unions were independently recomputed by sorting and merging device intervals clipped to Scheduler start→`scheduler_step_forward_submit_end`; this is neither FPM `wall_time_s` nor the FPM receive/emission timestamp. Correlation-linked Kernel duration remains a third, distinct quantity. `GPU Active` is 100% at every 1-ms point for GA10x and is not a useful Busy/Idle classifier; use the CUDA Kernel-interval union for observed busy time, but do not infer an exhaustive physical GPU idle budget from the absence of Kernel records (e.g. copy-engine work can be active without SM Kernel activity). The 1-ms metric series is whole GPU and therefore includes asynchronous work from other streams/workers; step-window selection is scheduler-aligned, not an exclusive per-RID hardware-counter attribution.
